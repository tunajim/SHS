About Students Helping Students

Students Helping Students (SHS) is a student-led organization at Washington State University Vancouver that provides peer-to-peer support for students. Our mission is to create a supportive community where students can help each other navigate the challenges of university life.


How we Started

SHS was established in 2018 by student employees. The program was created to fill gaps in tech services provided to students. We seek to provide Washington State University, Vancouver students with resources and support to help them succeed, while also providing our student techs more opportunities to gain experience in the field of technology.


Whether you're struggling with coursework, feeling overwhelmed, or just looking for a friendly face on campus, SHS is here for you. We believe that every student deserves support and encouragement, and we're committed to fostering a positive and inclusive environment at WSU Vancouver.
